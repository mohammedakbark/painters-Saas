# Hook formulas

These hooks are adapted from public Instagram skills (instagram-agent-skill's scored hook formulas, reels-scripting). The first line of a reel (0–3 s on screen) or caption must stop the scroll. Write **3 hooks**, then score each 1–5 on:
**Specific** (names a real pain or feature), **Audience** (a shop owner, painter or home customer recognises themselves), **Curiosity** (makes them want the next second), **Truth** (100% backed by the docs). Pick the highest total. If Truth is below 5, drop the hook.

| # | Formula | Example (paint shops) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pain question | "Still taking paint orders on WhatsApp?" |
| 2 | Stop doing X | "Stop writing painter dues in a notebook." |
| 3 | POV | "POV: you need 2 more buckets at the site." |
| 4 | Before / after | "Notebook vs app. Same shop. One week." |
| 5 | Number list | "3 things every paint shop owner asks us." |
| 6 | Myth vs fact | "Will other shops see my prices? No." |
| 7 | Audience call-out | "Painters in Kerala, this one's for you." |
| 8 | Hidden cost | "How much did that site *really* cost?" |
| 9 | First look / sneak peek | "First look at what we're building 👀" |
| 10 | Countdown / coming soon | "Coming soon to your paint shop." |
| 11 | Question to shops | "What if your customers could order from your shop on their phone?" |

Rules: keep it to 7 words or fewer on screen, use no clickbait the product can't back up, and never use a fake urgency date.
