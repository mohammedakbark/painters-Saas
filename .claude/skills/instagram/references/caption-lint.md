# Caption lint checklist

Run every check before marking a day `ready`. Fix the caption, then re-check. Report the result in `caption.md` under "Lint".

## Structure
- [ ] Line 1 is the hook and stands alone (≤ 125 characters, since that's what shows before "more").
- [ ] 3–5 short body lines; one idea per line; blank lines between blocks.
- [ ] Exactly one Malayalam line (natural, not machine-literal), marked for native review.
- [ ] One clear CTA from `brand.json` → `cta`.
- [ ] Ends with `Powered by @zero.byt`.
- [ ] Hashtags: 10–15, on their own block at the end, from `hashtags.md`. No banned or irrelevant tags.
- [ ] Total length ≤ 2,200 characters (Instagram limit). Aim for under 900.
- [ ] Emoji: at most 1 per line and at most 5 in total.

## Truth gate (must all pass)
- [ ] No product/app name. Use "a new app" or "the app", never an invented name.
- [ ] Every feature claim maps to a Confirmed, Confirmed direction or Implemented row in `docs/FEATURE_CATALOG.md`.
- [ ] Everything is framed as coming soon (no "available now", "download now" or store links).
- [ ] No prices, subscription plans, "free", discounts, launch dates, user/shop counts, or ratings.
- [ ] No GST/invoice, delivery, online payment, inventory/stock-count or tinting claims.
- [ ] Credit: always "approved by your shop", and never automatic, guaranteed or scored.
- [ ] Painter commission/incentive only in shop- or painter-facing posts, never as a perk for home customers.
- [ ] No claim that shops can see each other's customers, prices, credit or bills.
- [ ] No real customer, shop or painter data in visuals; screens use the fictional sample catalogue.
- [ ] No other brands' logos; paint brand names only if they appear in the sample data, which is fictional.
