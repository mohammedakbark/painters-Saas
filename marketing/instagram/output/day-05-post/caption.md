## Caption: poster B (F7)

Shop tip #1: write the shade code on every order. 🎨

Two shades can have nearly the same name.
The code is what stops a wrong tin leaving your counter.
In the app, the shade and its code stay saved on each order line, and your favourite shades are one tap away.

Save this tip and share it with your staff.
